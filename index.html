Who We Are

upNext is a career-focused AI & Data Science institute dedicated to delivering practical, research-oriented, and industry-aligned training. Founded by passionate software engineers, we believe learning should go beyond theory.

Our Approach Emphasizes:

  • Hands-on projects with real-world datasets
  • Modern AI tools and technologies
  • Portfolio development for career readiness
  • Personalized mentoring in small batches
  • Lab-based offline learning in Thrissur

Our Vision

To become a leading center of excellence in Applied Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, preparing students for the future of intelligent technologies.

Our Mission

Deliver high-quality, practical AI education that bridges the gap between academics and industry, equipping students with real-world problem-solving skills while promoting ethical and responsible AI practices.

Our Promise

We don't just train students — we transform them into professionals.
