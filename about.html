About upNext

Career-focused AI & Data Science education, built for the real world

Who We Are

upNext is a career-focused AI & Data Science institute dedicated to delivering practical, research-oriented, and industry-aligned training. Founded by passionate software engineers in Thrissur, we bring real-world industry experience into the classroom.

We believe learning should go beyond theory. Our programs emphasize hands-on projects, real-world datasets, modern AI tools, portfolio development, and career readiness.

Located in Thrissur, we offer hybrid learning with personalized mentoring and small batch sizes to ensure every student gets the attention they deserve.
