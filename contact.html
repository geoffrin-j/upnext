Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission process?

Simply fill out the contact form or call us. We'll schedule a counseling session to understand your goals and recommend the right program.

Do I need prior programming experience?

Not for beginner programs. Our Python + Data Analytics course is designed for complete beginners. Advanced programs require basic programming knowledge.

Are online classes available?

Yes! We offer both offline classes in Thrissur and live online options for all our programs.

What is the batch size?

We maintain small batch sizes (typically 10-15 students) to ensure personalized attention and mentoring.

Is placement assistance provided?

We provide comprehensive career preparation including resume building, interview prep, and portfolio development. We help you become job-ready.
